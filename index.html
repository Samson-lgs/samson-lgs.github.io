Final Year Project · Machine Learning · Medical Imaging

🧠 Brain Tumor Detection & Classification

My final year project is an automated brain MRI classification system that classifies scans into four categories: Glioma, Meningioma, No Tumor and Pituitary. The project preprocesses MRI images by resizing them to 160×160 pixels, normalizing pixel intensities and converting each image into a 76,800-feature vector. Five supervised machine-learning models — KNN, MLP, Decision Tree, Random Forest and Extra Trees — are trained and compared using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC and confusion-matrix analysis. On the evaluated test set, Extra Trees achieved 95.58% accuracy, followed by Random Forest at 94.29%.
